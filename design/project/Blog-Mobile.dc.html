How to Measure a Door or Window for a Copper Awning

Four measurements and two photos are all we need to size an awning.

By [Author] · [Month DD, YYYY] · 5 min read

Most calls start with "I'm not sure what size I need." Measure once with these steps, and we'll confirm the size with you before anything is cut.

1. Measure the width across the trim

Outside edge of the trim to outside edge. No trim? Measure the opening and tell us. Awnings usually overhang the trim by [X in, TBC] each side.

2. Measure the space above

Top of the trim to the nearest soffit, gutter or window above. Standard awnings are 24 or 30 inches high.

3. Note what's on the wall

Brick, stucco and siding take different fasteners. Note lights or house numbers near the trim.

4. Take two photos

One straight-on of the door or window and wall, one close-up with the tape across the trim. Email both to info@crescentcitycopper.com.

When to go custom

Between standard sizes, or tight above? We build to your numbers. Custom awnings can't be returned, so we confirm measurements by phone first.
