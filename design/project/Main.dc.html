Hand Made Custom Design

Are you looking for that special accent piece to dress up your windows or doors on your home’s exterior? If you answered yes – then a metal copper window awning or canopy from Crescent City Copper just might be the perfect solution!

Crescent City Copper specializes in the traditional art of creating handmade metal awnings, gutters, cupolas, chimney caps and other accessories from durable, natural copper. Each copper awning we make combines the best of old world craftsmanship with modern building techniques to create a beautiful, yet durable work of art that will look great on any style of exterior design from traditional to modern.

If you have any questions about what style, size or dimensions you need – please call us at 1-877-251-5327 and one of our staff members can answer your questions and help you choose.

We also offer a wide range of copper accessories for your home or business. This includes copper finials & cupolas, plus copper gutters and leader heads. We also offer other accessories and because we are sheet metal fabricators, we can create custom orders if you can’t find exactly what you need.

Thank you for visiting our website. We invite you to shop online and compare our prices on copper awnings which we believe are the best you will find anywhere!
