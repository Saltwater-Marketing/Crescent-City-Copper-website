Why a copper awning

Nothing complements a window or door quite like a copper awning. Not only do they provide a timeless look, awnings also offer year-round cover from the elements whether it’s southern heat or northern cold and ice. Copper is also known for its durability and low maintenance. Your copper awning will have no problem lasting you years without issue no matter what kind of climate you live in, and you won’t have to worry about cleaning it as copper awnings develop a natural patina over time.

These are the sizes and styles of awnings for both doors and windows that are most popular with our customers. We also carry a wide variety of brackets to choose from as well, so you can get the fit and look you want, no matter where on your home you’re planning on installing a copper awning.
