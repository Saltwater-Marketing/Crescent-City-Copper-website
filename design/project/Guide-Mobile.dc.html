What copper sheet is

Architectural copper sheet is nearly pure copper, rolled flat and sold by weight. It isn't painted or coated: what you see on installation day is the metal itself.

We cut every piece on computer-controlled equipment in our shop, then form the seams and edges. Nothing is outsourced.

How copper weathers

New copper is bright. It darkens to brown within months, then deeper brown. Over many years, faster in humid or salt air, a blue-green patina forms.

The patina protects the copper beneath it. It isn't the metal wearing away.

Durability and upkeep

Copper doesn't rust, and with no paint there's nothing to chip or repaint. Install it once and leave it alone.

Plan for runoff staining on light stone or stucco below, and keep copper away from galvanized steel and aluminum.

Copper in New Orleans

Our work is on homes and storefronts in the French Quarter, Garden District, Uptown, Old Metairie and the Northshore.

In a historic district? Check with the Vieux Carré Commission or the HDLC before you order. We can supply drawings for your application.
