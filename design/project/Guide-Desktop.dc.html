Why Choose Copper?

What copper sheet is

Architectural copper sheet is nearly pure copper, rolled flat and sold by weight per square foot. Unlike galvanized steel or aluminum, it isn't painted or coated. What you see on the day it's installed is the metal itself, and that's what will age.

We cut every piece from natural copper sheet on computer-controlled equipment in our shop, then form the seams and edges. Nothing is outsourced, so we can tell you exactly what went into your awning or cap.

How copper weathers

New copper is bright and pinkish-orange. Exposed to air and rain, it darkens to a warm brown within months, then to a deeper brown. Over many years, and faster in humid or salt air, a blue-green patina forms on the surface.

The patina isn't corrosion eating the metal. It's a thin layer that protects the copper beneath it, which is why old copper roofs and gutters keep working long after they turn green.

If you'd rather keep the bright color, ask us about clear coatings and what they need to stay clear. [Confirm whether CCC offers this.]

Durability and upkeep

Copper doesn't rust, and with no paint there's nothing to chip, peel or repaint. That's why it's used on historic roofs, steeples and domes that are expected to last generations. For an awning or chimney cap, the practical result is a piece you install once and leave alone.

Two things to plan for: copper runoff can stain light stone or stucco below it, and copper shouldn't touch galvanized steel or aluminum, which corrode where they meet it. We supply matched fasteners [TBC] and can advise on drip edges.

Copper in New Orleans

Copper awnings, gutters and lanterns are part of how the French Quarter, the Garden District and Uptown look. Our own work is on homes and storefronts in those neighborhoods, in Old Metairie and across the Northshore.

In a historic district? Exterior changes in the Vieux Carré and in local historic districts usually need approval from the Vieux Carré Commission or the HDLC. Check with them before you order; we can supply drawings and dimensions for your application.

Cost compared with other metals

Copper costs more up front than painted steel or aluminum. Over the life of the building it often costs less, because it never needs painting.

Standard or custom size

Standard sizes cover most single doors and windows, and you can order them online. For wide openings, odd shapes or commercial work, we build to your measurements or your architect's drawings. Custom-built pieces can't be returned, so we confirm measurements by phone before cutting.
